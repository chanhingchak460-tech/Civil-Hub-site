What Civil Hub is

Civil Hub is a personal revision library for civil engineering. Each subject has one page: a short orientation, the governing ideas, the formulas worth remembering, a worked example and a self-check. The aim is retrieval, not coverage.

The site was designed around a simple observation. Most students do not need another fifty pages to read; they need a short, reliable page that helps them find out what they can still reconstruct. Everything here is arranged to support that one act.

How the notes are made

The pages are written from the author's own coursework, laboratory reports and lecture material, then condensed and rewritten into plain English. Handwritten lecture notes, diagrams, worked problems and assignment feedback informed the structure of each page.

No lecture slides are reproduced, no institutional logos are used, and no personal data appears anywhere on the site. Formulas are re-typeset in plain notation, and diagrams are original line drawings made for this project.

Important limits

  • This is a supplementary revision aid. It does not replace regular lectures, laboratories, tutorials, field classes or official course materials.
  • It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to any university or professional body.
  • Notes are condensed by design. They may omit conditions, exceptions or local regulations that your course requires.
  • Worked examples are illustrative and use simplified numbers; they are not design guidance.
  • If anything here conflicts with your course material or your instructor's guidance, your course material wins.

Privacy

There are no accounts, no analytics and no cookies for tracking. The only thing stored is your revision progress, kept in your own browser's local storage. Clearing site data clears it. The site is a set of static files served from a public repository.

Design & type

The visual language borrows from Japanese editorial and architectural practice: warm paper, ink, hairline rules, generous margins and a single vermilion accent. Type is set in Zen Old Mincho and Zen Kaku Gothic New, with IBM Plex Mono for labels and Caveat for the margin notes. Illustrations are drawn as simple engineering line work.
